# HAUNTED.EXE: notes for whoever works on this next

A Halloween screensaver for Windows that runs in real VGA modes, written in C with no dependencies
beyond the standard Windows DLLs. Two scenes take turns: **HAUNT.EXE**, a possessed 486 PC in 80x25
text mode (720x400), and **CATACOMB**, a Catacomb 3-D / Wolfenstein-style crypt crawl in mode 13h
(320x200, 256 colours) that plays itself and that a person can take over by holding H for three
seconds. Everything is generated by the code: art, textures, music and effects (the caster's hand and
face and the cat merchant were traced from the owner's photographs).

Current release: **1.0.0b10** (pre-release, October 2026). Project page:
https://aspdesignlabs.github.io/HAUNTED.EXE/ · repo: `ASPDesignLabs/HAUNTED.EXE`.

## Working with the owner

- They play it by hand on their own Windows PC and report back. Mouse feel, sound and input capture
  have been confirmed by hand on real Windows. Anything new is untested by hand until they say so;
  say which parts were only tested in the harness or under Wine.
- For big features they want options and questions first ("bounce a few things off of me before we get
  started"), then they pick. Smaller decisions: pick a sensible default, say so, carry on.
- Honest status matters: the README's "What has been verified" and the page's "Beta status" table say
  exactly what was tested and how. Keep them true, including bad news (e.g. the wider crypt made the
  autopilot die more often; the numbers are in the README).
- British spelling in user-facing text (colour, armour). The README, page and in-game text have a
  plain, slightly dry, literary voice; match it. Code comments are short and in the same voice.
- No model identifiers in commits, code, comments or docs. Commit and PR attribution: follow whatever
  your environment specifies.

## Building and testing

```sh
make host                 # build/host: the harness (needs gcc, zlib)
make build/host_debug     # with -DHAUNT_DEBUG -DCRAWL_DEBUG: debug switches and event logging
make smoke                # both scenes, a scripted player, a 60 s benchmark (CI runs this)
make scr                  # dist/HAUNTED.scr (needs x86_64-w64-mingw32-gcc); make zip for the package
make cards                # title cards (needs Pillow)
sh tools/build_site.sh /tmp/site   # the page, as Pages will publish it
```

The harness (`tools/host.c`):

```sh
build/host shot  <haunt|crawl> <t1,t2,...> <prefix> [scale]   # PNGs; default scale 3 for 320x200, 1 for 720x400
build/host wav   <scene> <seconds> out.wav [sfx_id@time ...]   # sfx_id: the SFX_* number (SFX_SPLASH is 61)
build/host dump  <scene> <seconds> <prefix> <every_n_s>       # frames and audio together
build/host bench <scene> <seconds>                            # ms per frame, every frame drawn
build/host apl   <scene> <seconds>                            # average picture level; fast (draws twice a second), good for long sims
```

Environment for the harness: `HOST_INPUT="1:+H;4.3:-H;5:+W;7:-W;8:m400,0;9:+MOUSE1;9.2:-MOUSE1"`
(press `+`, release `-`, mouse counts `m`, wheel `w`, typed letters and digits `t`, as `6:tWORD`; key names
as in `core.h` without `KEY_`, so `CONSOLE`, `BACK`, `PGUP`, `PGDN` too),

`slot1.bin`..`slot3.bin`, the dead's mark as `mark.bin`), `BOTH=1` (scene rotation), `STAY=n`. A game
controller in `HOST_INPUT`: buttons `+PAD_A` ... `-PAD_RT` (names as `PAD_*` in `core.h`), sticks `aLX0.8`,
`aLY1`, `aRX-0.5`, `aRY0` (y up); any of them makes a pad plugged in. The harness prints `RUMBLE lo hi`
and `RUMBLE off` as the motors start and stop. `M` is the map's key (1.0.0b9).

Memory safety: build the harness with `-O1 -g -fsanitize=address,undefined -fno-omit-frame-pointer
-DHAUNT_DEBUG -DCRAWL_DEBUG` (same sources as `build/host`) and run long autopilot and scripted player
sessions with `ASAN_OPTIONS=detect_leaks=0`. Every round so far has been clean; keep it that way.

Windows under Wine (in a container with `wine` and `xvfb-run`): set `WINEPREFIX`, run `wineserver -w`
before `xvfb-run -a -s "-screen 0 1920x1080x24"` (a wineserver started outside the display gives black
screenshots), run `HAUNTED.scr /s`, wait about 25 s for it to come up, then drive it with XTest from
Python `ctypes` (`libXtst.so.6`: `XTestFakeKeyEvent`, `XTestFakeRelativeMotionEvent`) and take
screenshots with ImageMagick `import -window root`. Settings, progress, the ledger and the mark live under
`HKCU\Software\HalloweenVGASaver` (`wine reg query` to check).

## How CATACOMB works (the parts you will touch)

- **State.** `RUN` carries across levels, deaths and scene switches (level, hp, gold, souls, gear,
  shop flag). `G` is the level in play; `crawl_enter` clears it, `start_level` builds a level,
  `begin_shop` the merchant's room.
- **Generator** (`gen_level`). A plan of 11x11 blocks of 3x3 cells with one-cell walls between (plan
  grid `MG` = 23, map `MAPN` = 45, `mstart(m)` maps plan to map). Rooms span several blocks; halls are a
  maze of blocks whose stretches are drawn 1, 2 or 3 cells wide (`mz_widths`: a trunk from the start
  room to the farthest room is widest). Every hall keeps its middle **lane**: cells with x or y = 2
  (mod 4). Then: doors and colour-coded vaults (gold from level 1, silver from 3, crimson from 5) with
  keys, `place_secrets`, wall textures by room theme (`theme_tex`), colonnades, torches,
  `dress_level` (chains, hooks, gibbets, chandeliers, roots, web sheets, graveyards, fire pits, totems;
  every room gets a light), lightmap, fog.
- **Bosses** (docs/PLAN-b7.md; `BOSSES[]`, `BossDef`, `boss_of_level`; the fight in `update_boss`). The last
  level of each run (`crawl_boss`) holds its boss: the knight (10), the spider (20), the Deacon (30), the
  Wraith (40), then round again. `gen_level` keeps plan rows from `ARENA_PLAN` (cells from `BAND_Y0`, 29)
  out of the level (`K_PILLAR` on the plan), keeps a doorway at plan column `ARENA_DOOR_MX` open
  (`MCell.keep`), moves the start room as far from it as it can (`swap_rooms`), and stamps the boss's
  map last (`stamp_arena`, maps `MAP_LISTS`, `MAP_WEB`, `MAP_PULPIT`, `MAP_BOTTOM`: an antechamber, the arch,
  the arena; the legend is above `BOSSES`), so nothing of the level spills into it; `in_band` keeps the
  level's placements out. A boss is one of its run's creatures with `Mob.boss` (1 + `BOSS_*`): health in
  rank-I skeleton knights of the level (`BossDef.hp`), size, pace and blow. It waits untouchable
  (`BS_WAIT`) until you are a step into the arena; then the arch seals (`Door.locked` `SEALED`), its card
  shows (`G.bcard`), and its bar runs along the top (`render_bossbar`; toasts wait). At 75, 50, 25 and 10%
  (`WAVE_MARK`) `boss_hurt` stops its health at the mark and calls a wave (`BS_WAVE`): two seconds later
  `boss_adds` brings the wave's adds (`BossDef.waves`, `Mob.add`, at `add_rank`: the highest rank of each
  kind met since the last death, `RUN.top_rank`) out of its gates, burrows or heaps; when they are down it
  rises (`BS_RISE`) and fights on. Untouchable, it wears a pale shell (`DR_EDGE`) and spells ring dully on
  it (`G.bring`). Three fonts in the walls (`T_FONT`, `T_FONT_DRY`; `font_drink`, a third of your health)
  fill at the start and after the first and third waves. It falls (`boss_fall`): its scrip drops
  (`IT_SCRIP`, `SP_SCRIP`, to `RUN.scrip` at once), the exit (barred, `T_GATE`, while it lives) opens, the
  tally gains a BOSS row, the record (`Record.boss`) and achievements (`ACH_BOSS0`) note it, and on the way
  down `RUN.runs_done` counts the run. The autopilot (`boss_goal`) keeps three to six cells from it, away
  from its adds, drinks under 40% and takes the scrip; a boss level waits 1500 s, not 600, before it gives
  up on one. The bosses' own ways (runes, abilities) come in steps 5-8 of the plan.
- **The Ill-Fated Knight** (step 5). Sheds plate by waves, not health (`knight_stage`: full, then after
  wave 1 half, after wave 3 the breastplate), 15% quicker each time (`knight_pace`, which keeps the boss's
  pace). Drawn `DR_SHINE`: its plate's R_IRON raised, a glint sliding across, and while its Mirror Rune
  flashes, silver all over with a ring going out. Poses (`G.bpose`, `BP_PLANT` with `SP_KNIGHT_P0..2` for
  Rockfall, `BP_RAISE` then `BP_STRIKE` for the Sundering Arc) hold it still (`update_mob`'s boss hold).
  Swift Dodge (`knight_boss`, `knight_dash`, `dash_room`): 0.5 s untouchable (`G.bdash_t`; shots pass,
  `damage_mob` refuses), 2-5 cells, after-images from `G.bdash_x0/y0` for 0.8 s; to close in (5 s), to flee
  when `G.bhurt` (harm fading over 3 s) passes a tenth of its health or it stands in fire (9 s), aside from a
  shot (16 s). Mirror Rune (`knight_turns`): one missile in seven, not twice in 2 s, comes back as
  `MIS_TURNED` (`Bone.atk` the spell, drawn as it) at half damage, capped at a fifth of your health, with
  `SFX_MIRRORBELL`. The autopilot values missiles at 0.8 against it. Debug: `EVENT knight_dash close|flee|
  dodge`, `knight_turned`, `turned_hits`. A boss's hit radius grows with its scale.
- **The Wandering Spider** (step 6; `spider_boss`, `web_strike`, `egg_shot`). Sprites `SP_BSPIDER_A/B/LEAP`
  (the hunter's, a pale skull stamped on the back in `crawl_art.c`). Up to the ceiling every 4-7 s
  (`G.bsp_t`; `sub` 5 climbing, 0 overhead: out of reach, shots pass under, not `targetable`), across it to
  two cells short of you, down (`sub` 1) and a leap. Mend Rune: an eighth over 4 s (`G.bmend_t`) after a
  fifth lost since `G.bmend_base`, every 20 s at most, capped at the last wave mark, drawn `DR_MEND` (green).
  Tangled Web: rears 0.6 s (`G.web_rear`, the leap sprite), spits `MIS_WEB`; held, `slow_factor` is 0 and
  `cast_spell` sends each spell's damage into `G.web_hp` (a third of your health, fire double) instead;
  `render_web` draws the strands; next 12 s after you are free; the autopilot casts a fire bolt at nothing
  every 1.2 s while held and idle (`G.web_idle`). Egg Shot every 14-18 s (`G.bz2_t`): a sac (`Mob.small`) by
  a wall in your sight that hatches four small hunters 5 s later. Every 15 s one small hunter comes out of a
  burrow (`Mob.add` 2, four at most; only `add` 1 counts for a wave). Debug: `EVENT spider_ups`,
  `spider_mends`, `webbed`, `web_torn`, `egg_shots`. The BFS9000 takes 8% of a boss's health.
- **The Black Deacon** (step 7). Drawn with the deacon's rank-V (black) colours at twice the size. Bone
  Ward (`G.bward`, 4% of his health, `G.bward_cd` the Rare rune's cooldown to grow back): blows go to it
  first; six bone shards wheel about him while it holds. Grave Call is the deacon's chant from the
  chapel's heaps (`SP_BONES2` in his arena): one at a time, 12 s apart, three of his (`Mob.add` 3) about
  him at most; a tenth of his health in damage mid-chant (`G.bchant_dmg`) breaks it off. Litany (`BP_LITANY`,
  every 18-24 s): two seconds of green light (`DR_MEND`, the chant sprite), then a fifth of your health if he
  can see you; timber props (`K_PILLAR`) hide you, pews don't. Five chandeliers light the chapel. Neither he
  nor any boss's adds back away (`keeps_off`), and shamblers called as adds rise at once. Debug: `EVENT
  ward_broken`, `ward_grown`, `chant_broken`, `litany_hits`, `litany_missed`; `BOSS fight ...` every 10 s of a
  fight (health, ward, distance, sight, whether it is targetable and aimed at) and `BOSS adds left ...` when a
  wave's adds linger past 20 s.
- **The Wraith Unbound** (step 8; `wraith_boss` in place of `update_wraith`, `wraith_lit`, `wraith_open`,
  `brazier_set`, `brazier_facing`). Its arena's eight braziers (`G.bbz_*`) are props lit by a dynamic light
  each frame, so they can go out (`SP_BRAZIER_OUT`): it gutters one it passes within a cell, one in 8 s at
  most (`G.bgutter_cd`), and a void over one puts it out. E facing one kindles it in a second (`G.bbz_at`,
  `G.bbz_t`; walking off cancels); a wisp within 1.5 cells kindles it at once; the autopilot kindles any
  within two cells, and while fewer than four burn `boss_goal` takes it to the nearest one out. It can be
  hurt only within four cells of a lit brazier or three of a wisp, and not while in the stone
  (`wraith_open`: `damage_mob` refuses, shots pass, not `targetable`); it is drawn faint in the dark. The
  ordinary Wraith's kind has no weaknesses, so `weak_to` gives the boss 1, fire 1.2. Time Slip (`G.bslip_*`,
  the Rare rune's cooldown): a missile about to strike it, in light, slows to a fifth within three cells for
  1.5 s (`update_shots`), a grey ring (`DR_RIPPLE`) and `SFX_DEEPCHIME`, and it steps aside. Wraithwalk
  (`G.bww` 1 fading, 2 gone 1.5 s, 3 out of a wall two to three and a half cells from you; every 16-20 s).
  Its touch: 1.2 blows, every 2.5 s, chill and slow. Nothing drives it back. Debug: `EVENT brazier_out`,
  `brazier_lit`, `wraith_slips`, `wraithwalks`, `wraith_touches`. `in_arena` now stops short of the map's
  rim (it had none, and a Wraithwalk could read and land past the edge; found by ASan).
- **The caster's boss abilities** (step 9; `RUN.abil[b]` 0-3 by boss, `RUN.abil_sel` the right mouse's,
  `BA_NAME`, `BA_WAIT`, `ab_wait`, `ab_readied`; `G.ab_cd`, `ab_update`). Unlocked and raised at the **Hexed Totem**
  (`SP_HEXTOTEM` at `TOTEM_X/Y` in the merchant's back room, x 4-8, through an arch at (9,13); `UI_TOTEM`:
  `totem_rows`, `update_totemmenu`, `render_totemmenu`; a rank costs its number in that boss's scrip, `totem_extra`
  sells for `SCRIP_PRICE` 20,000, asked twice; the scrip hang on its hooks, drawn in `render_sprites`). **Swift
  Dodge**: W/A/S/D tapped twice within 0.25 s (`G.tap_key/tap_t`), `swift_dodge`, `dash_reach` (stops before walls,
  doors, props, creatures; under 2 cells nothing), `G.dash_t/dash_vx/vy` 0.25 s, `hurt_ex` refuses while it lasts,
  missiles pass (`passing`); the autopilot's `auto_dodge` lands on a lane cell 2-5 off. **Tangled Web**: a
  `SHOT_WEB` glob, `web_spit`, `web_land`, `web_holds` (not spirits, shades, wall skulls, sacs, web spiders or
  bosses), `Mob.webbed/webmax` (a third of your health a rank), `web_claw` (its `mob_blow` at half its rate),
  drawn `DR_WEBBED`; the wait is 2 s after a miss. **Grave Call**: `note_remains` keeps the last twelve remains of
  what you slew (`G.remains`), `remains_near` (aimed, or fresh for the autopilot), `grave_call` raises it as
  `Mob.ally` for `ally_t` (12/16/20 s), `update_ally` strikes the nearest foe or follows the hunt field to you;
  foes turn on the nearest within six cells (`ally_near`, `ally_hurt`, `Mob.acool`); allies are skipped by every
  spell, rune and count (`damage_mob` refuses them), drawn `DR_ALLY` (a violet edge). **Wraithwalk**: `wraithwalk`,
  `G.wwalk_t`; creatures lose you (`sees` and `fights` false, `alert_near` deaf), missiles pass, you walk through
  them; the view greys (`tint`) and the hand thins. Right mouse `use_readied`, Q `ready_next`; silenced, none of
  them. The sockets: `render_sockets` (`BA_MARK` glyphs, a filling line, pips for webs and risen dead, the
  readied one outlined), `sockets_shown`, `SOCK`; the readout moves over beside them. The autopilot
  (`auto_abilities`): dodges a missile or a warning, webs a creature come to blows, raises fresh remains in a
  fight, wraithwalks when cornered under 35%. Debug: `EVENT dodge`, `web_spit`, `web_hold`, `web_miss`,
  `web_free`, `gravecall`, `ally_falls`, `wraithwalk`, `totem_raise`, `totem_sell`.
- **The Accursed Portal** (step 10; `PORTAL_X/Y` (16,27), `T_PORTAL0..3` turning in `render_walls`, in the far wall
  of the grave plot: x 11-21, y 20-26, through an arch at (19,18) past the shrine; the stones come in step 12).
  `UI_PORTAL` (`portal_rows`, `update_portalmenu`, `render_portalmenu`): the runs opened (`RUN.run_top`, raised in
  `boss_fall` on the way down: the boss's level over ten) and the challenges (bosses slain, at `RUN.bstep`,
  `step_text`). `portal_run(k)`: the merchant's room before level 1 + 10k, as you are. `challenge_begin(b)`: the
  boss's level this time down (`boss_level_of`), `G.bchallenge`, the caster in the antechamber, the way down into
  it fallen in (rubble), the autopilot set leaving; `RUN.chal_from` is the level whose room it was begun from: the
  tally, a save and a scene switch all go back there; a death begins it again; `begin_shop` clears it. Debug:
  `EVENT portal_run`, `challenge`.
- **The autopilot in boss fights**: `find_target` takes a boss or its adds from any side within nine cells
  (before, only what was ahead or attacking, so a boss that never strikes, like the Deacon, was never
  aimed at); in a wave, `boss_goal` heads for the nearest add when none is within five cells.
- **The bosses' music** (`snd.c`: `BTHEMES`, `boss_step`; `snd_set_boss(theme, wave)`, `snd_boss_fall`).
  A slow waltz each on its own clock: eight bars of three beats, the last cut to two; a five-note
  ostinato that drifts against the bar; the bell on beat one; the tune rests one phrase in four and
  climbs an octave in another (the Wraith's voice sings a bar only now and then, `tune`). The knight's in
  D minor on brass, the spider's B-flat minor plucked with a skittering hat, the Deacon's G minor on the
  organ with a choir (`PAT_VOICE`), the Wraith's A minor on a drone. `boss_begin` starts it, `boss_wave`
  turns it up (at the next bar line: drums, the bass doubled, a semitone up, 8% quicker), the rise settles
  it, `boss_fall` fades it under three tolls; a death, a new level and the merchant's room fade it out.
  Every voice, drum and scheduled note belongs to a bus (`BUS_MARCH`, `BUS_MUZAK`, `BUS_BOSS`, `BUS_SFX`,
  set by `g_vbus` when it starts): the march gives way to the waltz as it does to a pocket's room, and the
  fanfare and the stinger (`BUS_SFX`) play over either.
- **Hazards and omens** (docs/PLAN-b7.md, section 6; `Hazard` in `G.haz`, `hz_new`, `update_hazards`,
  `boss_hazards`, `update_omens`). Each warns (`warn`, never under 0.9 s, `hz_warn`), harms (`on`) and fades
  (`off`), and hurts only the dweller, by a share of its health (`share`). `HZ_ROCK` (grit and a hardening
  shadow, then a rock: `HURT_STONE`, no dodging; rubble after), `HZ_FIRE` (Hellrent: cracks along `hz_line`
  in order, glowing up to the burst, fire a second, embers), `HZ_BILE` (a sheen creeping cell by cell from an
  outlet, foul once settled: poison and a fifth slower; drains when shut; `hz_bile_wet`), `HZ_VOID` (a ring and
  a thin column, then a purple volume: `HURT_VOID`, 60% pace, `G.silenced`: no spells or runes, the hand
  drops and greys, `snd_set_muffle`), `HZ_ARC` (the knight's Sundering Arc) and `HZ_GRASP` (the Wraith's
  Grasping Dark). The floor's marks are made each frame (`hz_marks`, `FMark`, `fm_pixel` in `render_floor`);
  what stands up is drawn with the sprites (`SP_GRIT0/1`, `SP_ROCK`, `SP_RUBBLE`, `SP_ERUPT0/1`, `SP_VOIDCOL`,
  `SP_HANDS`). `hz_dmap` (`hz_danger`) tells the autopilot which cells warn or harm: `decide` keeps out of them
  and it stops aiming to step out of one. A boss's own (`boss_hazards`): the knight's rockfall and arc, the
  spider's outlets (`G.bout_*`, from the map's `v` and the well, two open, one changing every 20 s, one more
  in a wave), the Deacon's lines, rings and crosses, the Wraith's voids and hands; each a fifth more often a
  challenge step. Omens (`omen_every`): the run's hazard near you, ahead if it can be, at half the harm, on
  its last four levels (every 90, 60, 40 and 30 s), never in a pocket or the arena; the Catacombs' bile
  comes from a wall turned to a grate (`T_GRATE`) for the while. The first of each kind is named
  (`RUN.omens`, saved).
- **Autopilot** walks only lanes (`apass`), with quick 90-degree turns; `decide`, `pick_goal`,
  `key_goal`. It remembers treasure it has seen, leaves at 270 s (300 if above 60% health) or 330
  (360), or earlier once it has most of the treasure. It presses about a third of the secrets it walks
  past. Setting off for the exit (`G.leaving`), it casts an exit wisp with 2 spirit or more. It stops
  at a Black Altar within 24 steps (30 to anoint) from the lane beside it (`altar_spot`, `at_altar`,
  `PS_ALTAR`): anoints when it is gold, wakes it with 25 spirit until 180 s (`WAKE_TIME`), sends its
  wisp on when the price leaves it 15 spirit, until 240 s (`wants_send`), and blesses with 40 when the
  level has undead until 240 s (`ALTAR_TIME`); while the altar's wisp is out and there is time it walks
  to the secret it seeks (`G.altar_seek` as `G.seek_secret`), and calls it home once the wall has moved
  and it is out of the room and within four cells of it.
- **The game controller** (docs/PLAN-pad.md). `Input` carries the pad (`pad`, `pad_down/pad_hit[PAD_*]`,
  `lx ly rx ry` past the dead zones, y up, `lt rt`); the platform fills it (`pad_poll`: every connected
  slot each frame, an empty one every 2 s; the program only while in front, the saver always; no pad
  press ever ends the saver; while a person plays with it, `SetThreadExecutionState` every 20 s and
  `SC_SCREENSAVE`/`SC_MONITORPOWER` refused). `app.c` takes any button held as it takes H (`A.hold_pad`
  picks the plate's words), keeps the rumble (`pad_rumble`, `app_pad_motors`) and `AppConfig.pad`
  (registry `Controller`). In the game, `pad_keys` (from `crawl_input`) turns the pad into the keys each
  place reads, by context: in a menu the D-pad and stick are W/S/A/D with repeat, A Enter, B and Menu
  Esc, Y Space, the bumpers A/D, View Tab; in play A is E, X Q, Y L, D-pad up M (the map, 1.0.0b9), View Tab, Menu Esc,
  D-pad left/right/down B/C/H, RT and LT the mouse buttons, the bumpers the wheel, B Swift Dodge by the
  stick's lean (`PADX.dodge`, done in `update_human`), the stick click runs (`PADX.run`); `human_walk`
  adds the sticks (`pad_turn`, `PAD_TURN`). What was held at the takeover counts once let go
  (`PADX.held`), and a direction that opened a menu isn't its first move (`PADX.menu`). `PADX.used` (the
  pad touched last, a key or the mouse turning it back) makes `pk` turn a hint's keys into buttons
  (`PK_KEY`/`PK_PAD`, face buttons marked `\1`), drawn by `hint8` in their colours (`hint_width`; `pkp`
  plain); only hint lines and prompts go through it, since a lone C or E elsewhere is prose. The
  controls card has a pad version, and `ab_text` the gifts' wording. `rumble()` (a person with the pad):
  hurts by share, death, a rock within 4 cells, a secret's slide, a boss's fall, the BFS9000.
- **Player mode** (`update_human`, `human_walk`, `human_use`, `human_cast`) after holding H. The hand
  is out of sight but for a cast or a ware reached for, and stays up 0.9 s after (`G.hand_base`,
  `G.ready_t`). Esc opens
  the question (flee, stay, sleepwalk, open the ledger); Tab the wardrobe (the
  seven worn slots and the bag, Enter moves a piece between them; A/D turns to the caster's totals and
  the spellbook); B the
  bestiary (`render_bestiary`: a page a kind, A/D through them, W/S through its ranks, then the
  achievements).
- **The record and achievements** (`Record` in `RUN.rec`, saved with the run, so each save and grave
  has its own). `rec_kill`, `rec_seen`, `rec_secret` and `rec_cleared` keep it, the autopilot's
  doings included. Achievements are bits (`ach_bit` layout: kind x rank, then secrets, levels,
  descents): 60 kills of a kind's lowest rank, then 40, 25, 15, 8; gilded 3 (`slayer_need`);
  secrets 1-50; each of levels 1-40 (`ach_level`: 21-40 from bit 256); each descent after the first
  (level 80, 120 ...). `toast` queues the
  pop-up at the upper right (`render_toasts`), with a progress note halfway.
- **Enemies** (`MOB_SKEL`, `MOB_GHOST` (the spectre: harmless, through walls, gone after level 10),
  `MOB_BAT`, `MOB_WSKULL` from level 3, `MOB_SHIELD` from 4, `MOB_SPIDER` from 6; guardians in vaults).
  `KINDS[]` gives each its name, rank family, first level and weaknesses (damage per spell, which
  `choose_spell` plays to). On its first level a kind comes alone (`may_spawn`).
  - **Ranks.** Every creature takes a rank on spawning (`pick_rank`, by the set's mix; `set_rank`):
    HP, damage, pace, souls and size by `RANK_*`, and its colour through `crawl_rank_lut` when drawn.
    From level 7, one level in three has a gilded creature that runs when hurt and drops treasure.
    Ranks II and up sometimes drop coin, gems or a potion (not counted in the level's treasure).
  - **Captions.** `name_newcomers` names each kind and rank the first time it is seen on a level.
  - **Strength.** HP is the kind's base x 1.04 per level x the rank's; `mob_damage()` is
    (4 + 0.8 x level) x the rank's, less 15% per death on the level (`G.retries`, up to 45%, reset
    when cleared). `throw_interval()` 5% quicker per level. Bones thrown from beyond 4 cells spread
    wider (wide halls made long throws too deadly).
  - **Hunting.** `build_hunt` keeps a field of steps to the caster. A creature that sees the caster,
    is hit, hears a spell within 8 steps or a footstep within 3 (`alert_near`, `footstep`; Quiet boots
    shorten the footstep's reach) is alerted (`Mob.alert`) and follows the
    field round corners; the dead pull ordinary doors open. Only the nearest three hunt at once
    (`pick_hunters`). A thrower that finds you again waits a moment before it throws.
  - **Residents.** Ossuaries and crypts have residents (`Mob.home`) that keep to their room until
    alerted. In player mode the "something wakes ahead" respawn waits 35 s of quiet, not 11.
  - **Kinds' properties** (`KF_*` in `KINDS`, tested with `KIND_IS`): undead, solid, spirit (nothing
    hurts it), unique, opens doors, melee, uncounted. A newcomer's first level always brings one.
  - **The newcomers so far.**
    - The shambler (from 2) and the Drowned (from 7) wait under turned earth before a grave, or a
      stirring puddle (`place_risers`, `update_riser`), and rise when you come near. They grab, which
      slows you; the Drowned burst into a foul pool (`Pool`).
    - Bone hounds (from 8) come in pairs (`spawn_wanderer`), bite and dart back.
    - The Wraith (from 9; `update_wraith`) comes once a level after 240 s (`CRAWL_WRAITH=s` to
      test), from the far end, down the hunt field, opening doors, while torches gutter near it.
      It can't be killed: fire, a wisp's light close by and Dread drive it back for 5 s
      (`wraith_back`). The autopilot turns fire on it.
    - The hunting spider (from 11) waits upside down on the ceiling (`DR_FLIPV`), drops when you
      come close, and leaps (`start_leap`, `update_leap`); one leap in three poisons.
    - The ghoul (from 12) leaps too, and feeds on bones when it has lost you (`sub` 4: it mends, and
      takes double damage).
    - The skeleton knight (from 13, with two hounds after its first level): a 0.9 s wind-up and a
      heavy blow; `knight_stage` (by health) picks its plate, its pace, and whether fire bites.
    - The brood mother (from 14) lays egg sacs (`MOB_SAC`, `update_sac`) that split into spiderlings
      (`MOB_LING`); the sacs die with the last mother.
    - The shade (from 16) shows only in light (`shade_alpha`, `shade_hidden`: torches, fires, the
      wisp's pool, a spell going by, and for a moment after its touch); its touch takes souls, or
      more health when there are none. The autopilot can't target it in the dark.
    - Ember imps (from 17) start beside magma (`place_imps`), keep their distance and lob embers
      (`MIS_EMBER`) that burn the floor where they land (`Burn.foe`).
    - The deacon (from 18) walks with two or three skeletons, keeps back, and chants over remains

   GraphQL-based `gh` commands like `gh release view` are blocked in cloud sessions), download each asset,
   and fetch the live page.

## Known issues and loose ends

- With the items (step 7 of the items plan), eight 50-minute autopilot runs from level 1 died 3 times
  (7 at 1.0.0b3; six of eight never died in both), levels 8-9 reached. Weak runs were the ones whose
  shopping skipped attack spells, so `shop_choose` takes three attack spells first, and saves only for a
  spell within two levels' gold (`gear_haul` x 0.6 x 2).
- Secrets, the graves, the items, spirit, bones, the merchant's room and the Black Altars have not been
  played by hand on Windows yet; nor has anything in 1.0.0b5 (the sliding bar, wisps in the crypt, the L
  list, the altar's menu and blessing, the console), nor anything in 1.0.0b6 (chains and pockets, the
  Backrooms and Severance, coins, the shrine, runes by quality).
- With 1.0.0b5's wisps and altars, eight runs on each start died 4 times from level 1 (3 at 1.0.0b4), 11
  from level 11 (12) and 28 from level 16 (25). The Wraith had come only once a session since 1.0.0b3;
  now that it comes once a level, it is behind most of the Deep's extra deaths.
- With 1.0.0b6's chains (both builds run again on the same seeds; the event log counts a death in a
  pocket as `died` too, followed by `pocket_thrown`): in the crypt 4 deaths from level 1 (5 at 1.0.0b5),
  7 from level 11 (11), 30 from level 16 (28), plus 2, 2 and 5 in chains. The autopilot reaches few

  eight runs (it bought 20 from the merchant at 1.0.0b5); it never saves the six coins to raise it.
  Exquisite runes (only through the debug switches so far) made level 18 deathless; untuned.
- Spectres in a pocket come back when you go in again: they can't be killed, so they never get a slain
  bit (`PK.slain`). Harmless, but worth knowing when counting creatures.
- `decay_tex` already draws cracks on ordinary walls at depth; keep secret cracks distinguishable if
  textures change (see the plan).
- CI warns that `actions/download-artifact@v6` targets Node 20; bump the action when a newer major is out.
- Past level 40 the runs come round with a numeral on the names; no loop moods yet, and the bosses are
  stronger only as their level makes them.
- With 1.0.0b7, eight 100-minute dream runs from level 1 (seeds 1-15 odd, against the step-10 build on
