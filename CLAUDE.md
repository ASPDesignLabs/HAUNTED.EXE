# HAUNTED.EXE: notes for whoever works on this next

A Halloween screensaver for Windows that runs in real VGA modes, written in C with no dependencies
beyond the standard Windows DLLs. Two scenes take turns: **HAUNT.EXE**, a possessed 486 PC in 80x25
text mode (720x400), and **CATACOMB**, a Catacomb 3-D / Wolfenstein-style crypt crawl in mode 13h
(320x200, 256 colours) that plays itself and that a person can take over by holding H for three
seconds. Everything is generated by the code: art, textures, music and effects (the caster's hand and
face and the cat merchant were traced from the owner's photographs).

Current release: **1.0.0b3** (pre-release, October 2026). Project page:
https://aspdesignlabs.github.io/HAUNTED.EXE/ · repo: `ASPDesignLabs/HAUNTED.EXE`.

The next planned work (themes every five levels, wall tiles, enemies, bosses, bonus levels) is written
up in [`docs/PLAN-descent.md`](docs/PLAN-descent.md). The creatures, their colour ranks and the order
in which creatures and decorations appear are in [`docs/PLAN-bestiary.md`](docs/PLAN-bestiary.md),
which replaces the descent plan's section on enemies. The owner has accepted the recommended answer
to every question in both; the bestiary's status line says how far the work has got. Read both
before starting on any of it. The overhaul of items, abilities, spirit and the merchant is planned in
[`docs/PLAN-items.md`](docs/PLAN-items.md); the owner has answered its questions, and its status line
says which of its seven steps are built (steps 1 to 5 so far: items as data, save version 3,
everything worn felt in the game, spirit and bones, the abilities, and the merchant).

## Working with the owner

- They play it by hand on their own Windows PC and report back. Mouse feel, sound and input capture
  have been confirmed by hand on real Windows. Anything new is untested by hand until they say so;
  say which parts were only tested in the harness or under Wine.
- For big features they want options and questions first ("bounce a few things off of me before we get
  started"), then they pick. Smaller decisions: pick a sensible default, say so, carry on.
- Honest status matters: the README's "What has been verified" and the page's "Beta status" table say
  exactly what was tested and how. Keep them true, including bad news (e.g. the wider crypt made the
  autopilot die more often; the numbers are in the README).
- British spelling in user-facing text (colour, armour). The README, page and in-game text have a
  plain, slightly dry, literary voice; match it. Code comments are short and in the same voice.
- No model identifiers in commits, code, comments or docs. Commit and PR attribution: follow whatever
  your environment specifies.

## Building and testing

```sh
make host                 # build/host: the harness (needs gcc, zlib)
make build/host_debug     # with -DHAUNT_DEBUG -DCRAWL_DEBUG: debug switches and event logging
make smoke                # both scenes, a scripted player, a 60 s benchmark (CI runs this)
make scr                  # dist/HAUNTED.scr (needs x86_64-w64-mingw32-gcc); make zip for the package
make cards                # title cards (needs Pillow)
sh tools/build_site.sh /tmp/site   # the page, as Pages will publish it
```

The harness (`tools/host.c`):

```sh
build/host shot  <haunt|crawl> <t1,t2,...> <prefix> [scale]   # PNGs; default scale 3 for 320x200, 1 for 720x400
build/host wav   <scene> <seconds> out.wav [sfx_id@time ...]
build/host dump  <scene> <seconds> <prefix> <every_n_s>       # frames and audio together
build/host bench <scene> <seconds>                            # ms per frame, every frame drawn
build/host apl   <scene> <seconds>                            # average picture level; fast (draws twice a second), good for long sims
```

Environment for the harness: `HOST_INPUT="1:+H;4.3:-H;5:+W;7:-W;8:m400,0;9:+MOUSE1;9.2:-MOUSE1"`
(press `+`, release `-`, mouse counts `m`, wheel `w`; key names as in `core.h` without `KEY_`),
`HOST_SAVE=file` / `HOST_LOAD=file` (progress, stands in for the registry), `HOST_SLOTS=dir`
(graves as `slot1.bin`..`slot3.bin`), `BOTH=1` (scene rotation), `STAY=n`.

Debug build switches (`CRAWL_DEBUG`): `CRAWL_SEED=n` (seeds are OR-ed with 1, so 2 and 3 are the
same), `CRAWL_LEVEL=n` (start deeper; you start at the merchant), `CRAWL_GEAR=t` (every spell and
rune at tier t, and an Uncommon piece of level 2t in every slot), `CRAWL_BAG=q` (a bag of quality q, all
but one place filled with spares), `CRAWL_ABIL=a` (every worn piece with ability a, the `AB_*` number), `CRAWL_GOLD`, `CRAWL_SOULS`, `CRAWL_MAP=1` (prints the map, the plan and a lane
reachability check), `CRAWL_MAPAT=t`, `CRAWL_TPSECRET=n` / `CRAWL_TPEXIT=1` (stand in front of a secret
or the exit once you take control), `CRAWL_WISP=1` (wisp from the start), `CRAWL_FOLLOWWISP=1` (a
player that walks wherever the wisp leans: a test that it leads out), `CRAWL_SHOWHAND`, `CRAWL_BFSANY`,
`CRAWL_RANK=r` (every creature at rank r), `CRAWL_WRAITH=s` (the Wraith at s seconds), `CRAWL_MEET=k`
(one creature of kind k, the `MOB_*` number, a few cells ahead of the caster five seconds into each level).
`HAUNT_DEBUG` adds `HAUNT_PLAN=10,11,...` to force HAUNT.EXE acts (numbers in the README).

The debug build prints the autopilot's state once a second (`lvl=... t=... hp=... items=a/b val=a/b
...` plus a second line of hit counters) and `EVENT` lines: `cleared level N t=..`, `died level N
t=..`, `secret i of n`, `key..`, `leaving`, `bought`, `shop_pick`, `cast`, `defense`, `exit_seen`,
`control`. Long simulations are just `CRAWL_SEED=s timeout 3000 build/host_debug apl crawl 3000 >
log` per seed, four at a time, then a short script over the logs: levels reached, median level time
(from `cleared`), treasure share (`val=` on the last state line before `cleared`), deaths, purchases,
secrets. Compare against a build of the previous commit (a `git worktree`) on the same seeds; one run
can spiral, so use at least eight seeds before trusting a balance change.

A save blob (for `HOST_LOAD`) is `"HVS1"` (u32 0x31535648 LE), version 3, then little-endian int32
level, hp, gold, souls (in tenths), shop-first flag, then the gear packed field by field (`gear_pack`:
attack and rune tiers, the seven worn pieces, bag size and quality, five bag places, bones, a held ware;
a piece is 24 bytes, `PIECE_BYTES`), then the run's `Record` (`rec_pack`: u16 kills by kind and rank,
u8 kills by kind and spell, u8 ranks seen by kind, u16 secrets, u16 descents, u32 levels cleared, 32
bytes of achievement bits; `REC_BYTES` in all): 939 bytes. Versions 1 and 2 (the old 46-byte raw
`Gear`; version 1 without a record) still load: their worn robe, hood and best ring become Common
pieces of their tier (`gear_from_old`). A few lines of Python writing version 1 are still the quickest
way to start a test at a given level with given spells.

Memory safety: build the harness with `-O1 -g -fsanitize=address,undefined -fno-omit-frame-pointer
-DHAUNT_DEBUG -DCRAWL_DEBUG` (same sources as `build/host`) and run long autopilot and scripted player
sessions with `ASAN_OPTIONS=detect_leaks=0`. Every round so far has been clean; keep it that way.

Windows under Wine (in a container with `wine` and `xvfb-run`): set `WINEPREFIX`, run `wineserver -w`
before `xvfb-run -a -s "-screen 0 1920x1080x24"` (a wineserver started outside the display gives black
screenshots), run `HAUNTED.scr /s`, wait about 25 s for it to come up, then drive it with XTest from
Python `ctypes` (`libXtst.so.6`: `XTestFakeKeyEvent`, `XTestFakeRelativeMotionEvent`) and take
screenshots with ImageMagick `import -window root`. Settings, progress and graves live under
`HKCU\Software\HalloweenVGASaver` (`wine reg query` to check).

## How CATACOMB works (the parts you will touch)

- **State.** `RUN` carries across levels, deaths and scene switches (level, hp, gold, souls, gear,
  shop flag). `G` is the level in play; `crawl_enter` clears it, `start_level` builds a level,
  `begin_shop` the merchant's room.
- **Generator** (`gen_level`). A plan of 11x11 blocks of 3x3 cells with one-cell walls between (plan
  grid `MG` = 23, map `MAPN` = 45, `mstart(m)` maps plan to map). Rooms span several blocks; halls are a
  maze of blocks whose stretches are drawn 1, 2 or 3 cells wide (`mz_widths`: a trunk from the start
  room to the farthest room is widest). Every hall keeps its middle **lane**: cells with x or y = 2
  (mod 4). Then: doors and colour-coded vaults (gold from level 1, silver from 3, crimson from 5) with
  keys, `place_secrets`, wall textures by room theme (`theme_tex`), colonnades, torches,
  `dress_level` (chains, hooks, gibbets, chandeliers, roots, web sheets, graveyards, fire pits, totems;
  every room gets a light), lightmap, fog.
- **Autopilot** walks only lanes (`apass`), with quick 90-degree turns; `decide`, `pick_goal`,
  `key_goal`. It remembers treasure it has seen, leaves at 270 s (300 if above 60% health) or 330
  (360), or earlier once it has most of the treasure. It presses about a third of the secrets it walks
  past.
- **Player mode** (`update_human`, `human_walk`, `human_use`, `human_cast`) after holding H. Esc opens
  the question (flee, stay, sleepwalk, carve a gravestone, rise from a grave); Tab the wardrobe (the
  seven worn slots and the bag, Enter moves a piece between them; A/D turns to the caster's totals and
  the spellbook); B the
  bestiary (`render_bestiary`: a page a kind, A/D through them, W/S through its ranks, then the
  achievements).
- **The record and achievements** (`Record` in `RUN.rec`, saved with the run, so each save and grave
  has its own). `rec_kill`, `rec_seen`, `rec_secret` and `rec_cleared` keep it, the autopilot's
  doings included. Achievements are bits (`ach_bit` layout: kind x rank, then secrets, levels,
  descents): 60 kills of a kind's lowest rank, then 40, 25, 15, 8; gilded 3 (`slayer_need`);
  secrets 1-50; each of levels 1-20; each descent after (level 40, 60 ...). `toast` queues the
  pop-up at the upper right (`render_toasts`), with a progress note halfway.
- **Enemies** (`MOB_SKEL`, `MOB_GHOST` (the spectre: harmless, through walls, gone after level 10),
  `MOB_BAT`, `MOB_WSKULL` from level 3, `MOB_SHIELD` from 4, `MOB_SPIDER` from 6; guardians in vaults).
  `KINDS[]` gives each its name, rank family, first level and weaknesses (damage per spell, which
  `choose_spell` plays to). On its first level a kind comes alone (`may_spawn`).
  - **Ranks.** Every creature takes a rank on spawning (`pick_rank`, by the set's mix; `set_rank`):
    HP, damage, pace, souls and size by `RANK_*`, and its colour through `crawl_rank_lut` when drawn.
    From level 7, one level in three has a gilded creature that runs when hurt and drops treasure.
    Ranks II and up sometimes drop coin, gems or a potion (not counted in the level's treasure).
  - **Captions.** `name_newcomers` names each kind and rank the first time it is seen on a level.
  - **Strength.** HP is the kind's base x 1.04 per level x the rank's; `mob_damage()` is
    (4 + 0.8 x level) x the rank's, less 15% per death on the level (`G.retries`, up to 45%, reset
    when cleared). `throw_interval()` 5% quicker per level. Bones thrown from beyond 4 cells spread
    wider (wide halls made long throws too deadly).
  - **Hunting.** `build_hunt` keeps a field of steps to the caster. A creature that sees the caster,
    is hit, hears a spell within 8 steps or a footstep within 3 (`alert_near`, `footstep`; Quiet boots
    shorten the footstep's reach) is alerted (`Mob.alert`) and follows the
    field round corners; the dead pull ordinary doors open. Only the nearest three hunt at once
    (`pick_hunters`). A thrower that finds you again waits a moment before it throws.
  - **Residents.** Ossuaries and crypts have residents (`Mob.home`) that keep to their room until
    alerted. In player mode the "something wakes ahead" respawn waits 35 s of quiet, not 11.
  - **Kinds' properties** (`KF_*` in `KINDS`, tested with `KIND_IS`): undead, solid, spirit (nothing
    hurts it), unique, opens doors, melee, uncounted. A newcomer's first level always brings one.
  - **The newcomers so far.**
    - The shambler (from 2) and the Drowned (from 7) wait under turned earth before a grave, or a
      stirring puddle (`place_risers`, `update_riser`), and rise when you come near. They grab, which
      slows you; the Drowned burst into a foul pool (`Pool`).
    - Bone hounds (from 8) come in pairs (`spawn_wanderer`), bite and dart back.
    - The Wraith (from 9; `update_wraith`) comes once a level after 240 s (`CRAWL_WRAITH=s` to
      test), from the far end, down the hunt field, opening doors, while torches gutter near it.
      It can't be killed: fire, the wisp's light close by and Dread drive it back for 5 s
      (`wraith_back`). The autopilot turns fire on it.
    - The hunting spider (from 11) waits upside down on the ceiling (`DR_FLIPV`), drops when you
      come close, and leaps (`start_leap`, `update_leap`); one leap in three poisons.
    - The ghoul (from 12) leaps too, and feeds on bones when it has lost you (`sub` 4: it mends, and
      takes double damage).
    - The skeleton knight (from 13, with two hounds after its first level): a 0.9 s wind-up and a
      heavy blow; `knight_stage` (by health) picks its plate, its pace, and whether fire bites.
    - The brood mother (from 14) lays egg sacs (`MOB_SAC`, `update_sac`) that split into spiderlings
      (`MOB_LING`); the sacs die with the last mother.
    - The shade (from 16) shows only in light (`shade_alpha`, `shade_hidden`: torches, fires, the
      wisp's pool, a spell going by, and for a moment after its touch); its touch takes souls, or
      more health when there are none. The autopilot can't target it in the dark.
    - Ember imps (from 17) start beside magma (`place_imps`), keep their distance and lob embers
      (`MIS_EMBER`) that burn the floor where they land (`Burn.foe`).
    - The deacon (from 18) walks with two or three skeletons, keeps back, and chants over remains
      or bone heaps (`DEACON_CHANT`) until a skeleton gets up from them, four at most. The autopilot
      aims at it first.
    - Gargoyles (from 19) are statues on plinths (`place_gargoyles`, `garg_wake`) until you pass
      within three cells or hit one; then they fly over the floor's things (`blocks_flight`) and swoop.
- **Secrets** (`place_secrets`, `open_secret`, `update_slide`). One on levels 1-2, two on 3-5, three
  from 6. A solid plan block beside a hall becomes a 3x3 closet behind a wall (`Cell.secret` 1 closet,
  2 wall); the wall's cracks are drawn by the renderer only within about two cells. E/Space pushes it
  three cells back over 2.3 s (`SFX_PUSHWALL`, `SFX_SECRET`, "A SECRET! n OF m"). Loot: treasure and
  often a potion, never a key, flagged `Item.secret` and kept out of the level's totals. The tally has a
  SECRETS row.
- **The will-o'-wisp** (`update_wisp`, `render_wisp`, `wisp_goal`, `wisp_heading`, `build_route`).
  After `WISP_SOULS` (10) souls on a level. A BFS field to the goal: the exit's doorstep, or first the
  key to the vault the exit is in (and to the vault that key is in). It leans to the farthest point
  along the route with a clear walk (`walk_clear`, body-width, props count). Drawn in screen space,
  tethered to the back of the hand (`WISP_AX/AY`); the autopilot raises the hand to carry it. Lights
  four cells round with a flat pool (`light_dynamic_ex(..., flat)`). Reset per level.
- **Rendering.** Raycaster over the cell map with a sliding secret box, floor/ceiling casting, a
  lightmap (`LMR` texels per cell; static lights cached, dynamic lights per frame; `torch_flicker`, phase
  below -100 means steady), fog, sprites with sway, the hand, HUD (Wolfenstein status bar for a player,
  a small readout for the autopilot).
- **Art** (`crawl_art.c`). The 256-colour palette is fully allocated in 16-entry ramps (`R_STONE` ...
  `R_GLOW`, `C_WHITE`, 13 gold, `C_CLEAR`); `R_FLAME` and `R_GLOW` are full-bright.
- **Sets** (`crawl.h`: `crawl_set`, `crawl_step`, `crawl_loop`, `crawl_decay`, `crawl_depth`). Levels
  come in four sets of five (the Crypts, the Catacombs, the Hollows, the Deep); the fifth of each is
  meant for its boss, and past level 20 the sets repeat. Each set has two tints that the stone, floor
  and ceiling move between over its five levels. Decay (`decay()`: grime, moss, drips and cracks by
  `decay_tex`, and more of the dressing) starts fresh at each set and reaches 1 on its last level.
  Depth (`depth()`: fewer torches, thicker fog, a darker base light, older bones) grows set by set and
  stays at 1 from the Deep on. `level_name` gives each of the twenty its name, with a numeral after
  the first time down. `crawl_art_level` regenerates the wall textures with a level seed. Textures
  and sprites are 64x64, column-major.
- **Ranks and the bestiary art.** `crawl_rank_lut[family][rank]` recolours a creature's body ramp
  (`PF_*`, `RK_*` in `crawl.h`). The new creatures' and decorations' sprites are drawn in
  `crawl_art.c`; `make sheets` shows them. See `docs/PLAN-bestiary.md` for what is in play.
- **Saves.** `crawl_save`/`crawl_load` (format above; the gear is packed field by field, so adding to
  `Gear` means adding to `gear_pack`/`gear_unpack` and a new version that still loads the old ones;
  `REC_KINDS` is fixed at 24 because the record is packed by it). Progress saves on fleeing; graves are
  the same blob plus 8 bytes of time in slots 1-3 (`SLOT_MAX` 2048).
- **Items** (`crawl_gear.c`, docs/PLAN-items.md). A `Piece` has a kind (`IK_*`: bracer, boots,
  amulet, hood, robes, ring, bag), a quality (`Q_*`, white to gold: `QUAL_RAMP`), its level, Anointed or
  not, an ability (`AB_*`) and up to six rolled stats (`ST_*`, in tenths; health restored in
  hundredths). `piece_roll` follows the plan's tables (`KIND_STATS`, `QUAL_STATS`, `ABIL_CHANCE`,
  `stat_base`); no piece rolls past its stat's cap (`STAT_CAP`). `gear_total` sums what is worn, lets
  Swiftness and Arcane Knowledge raise the summed stat once, then caps it. `piece_name` gives the two
  name lines; `piece_worth` and `gear_value` are the autopilot's sense of what helps (it counts only stats
  and abilities the game uses so far, `FELT`, and never buys a bag); `gear_take` wears
  a piece if it is better and bags or loses the old one (`take_piece` says which in a caption).
  In play: Armor, Dodge, Health, Restores; the resists (poison and slowing last shorter and bite less,
  `slow_factor`; burning hurts less); Movement (and Swiftness); Quiet (`footstep`: each step alerts
  creatures three hunt-field steps off, less with Quiet); Spell Haste; Damage; the elements
  (`hit_mult`: each adds its share of every spell's damage, by the creature's weakness to Fire Bolt,
  Frost Shard or Chain Lightning); Spell Effect (`spell_effect`: Soul Drain's healing, Frost's slow,
  Hellfire's burning floor and every rune's strength); Thorns; Spirit Cost (below). The abilities
  (`update_abilities` and where each acts): Swiftness and Arcane Knowledge raise their stat's total;
  Treasure Hunter adds to treasure picked up and whispers from an unopened secret wall within three
  cells; Keymaker makes keys within its reach sparkle through walls while the wisp is lit
  (`DR_GLINT`), and L (`KEY_L`, `wisp_choose`) turns the wisp from the exit to each key still lying,
  in colour order (`G.wisp_key`); Commune shows creatures within its reach as red shapes through the
  walls every 15 s (`DR_COMMUNE`); Thorns hurts what strikes you; Mirrored may send a missile back,
  homing on its thrower round corners (`Bone.from`, `reflect_field`). The hand shows the robe on the sleeve, the right bracer
  as an iron cuff inlaid in its colour, and the ring's stone; the hood frames the portrait. Tab's
  second page lists every total, gold for what an ability adds, blue at a cap (`gear_capped`).

- **Spirit and bones.** Spirit is the souls (`RUN.souls` and `RUN.soul_frac`, `spirit()`), spent to
  the tenth by `spend_spirit` and shown with one decimal (`spirit_text`). In `cast_spell`: tier 1 and
  below (and the starting fireball) is free; above, `spell_cost` (damage over 150, 0.1 to 1, less Spirit
  Cost; Seeker Wisps 1 a wisp); with too little, the cast goes at tier 1 (`Shot.tier` carries it to the
  hit) and a caption says so once a level (`G.spirit_said`). Soul Drain's healing costs `heal_cost` a
  point (half a soul at tier 1, a quarter at 11) or doesn't happen. The BFS9000 keeps its price in
  souls. Every spear takes a bone (`Gear.bones`, up to `BONES_MAX` 30; the spear brings `SPEAR_BONES`
  6 when bought or improved); skeletons, shield-bearers, knights and hounds drop them (`IT_BONE`, about
  one in eight, more by rank). The status bar shows spirit, and the bones over the spear. The autopilot
  weighs each spell at the strength it can pay for, grows thrifty under 15 spirit and skips spears
  without bones (`choose_spell`). The debug build logs `EVENT weak_<spell>` for each weak cast, `EVENT commune` and
  `EVENT mirrored`.

- **The merchant** (`gen_shop`, `begin_shop`, `update_shop`, `shop_human`). A room 11 cells wide
  with five to seven pedestals in an arc (`G.ped_x/ped_y`, `gear_shop_pedestals`): a spell, a rune,
  the rest things to wear, a bag at most once a visit and only one bigger than yours. One to three
  wares a visit (`gear_shop_purchases`, `G.shop_left`); the autopilot buys up to that by value,
  keeping 15% of a level's haul back after the first. A person: E buys and wears it at once
  (`gear_take_wear`); with no bag a worn slot gets a red warning on the card first
  (`G.shop_confirm`, a second E buys); with a full bag `UI_LETGO` asks which spare to let go. Q holds
  a ware for a quarter of its price (`Gear.held`, `G.shop_held`); next visit it is back on its
  pedestal at the price it showed (`G.offer_back`), and the hold ends when the caster leaves the room.
  Price tags over the pedestals skip any that would cover a nearer one. The debug build logs
  `EVENT shop_look` as a person's eye moves between wares.

## Releasing

1. Update `README.md` (features, "What has been verified" with real numbers), `site/index.html` (the
   page describes the released build only, so change it in the release commit), `.github/release-notes.md`,
   and any preview in `docs/` that shows outdated things (frames are 640x400 = native pixels doubled,
   6 px black gaps).
2. Bump `VERSION` (e.g. `1.0.0b3`) and push to `main`. CI builds, smoke-tests and creates release
   `v<VERSION>` (a, b or rc makes it a pre-release) with `HAUNTED.EXE`, `HAUNTED.scr`, the zip and
   `SHA256SUMS.txt`; Pages redeploys. Pushing tags from an agent session is dropped by the proxy, so let
   CI make the tag.
3. Check: `gh run list`, the release through the REST API (`gh api repos/ASPDesignLabs/HAUNTED.EXE/releases/tags/v...`;
   GraphQL-based `gh` commands like `gh release view` are blocked in cloud sessions), download each asset,
   and fetch the live page.
4. The X card URL carries the version, so a new version refreshes link previews.

## Known issues and loose ends

- In the wider crypt the autopilot dies more than it did in 1.0.0b1's narrow halls (eight 50-minute
  runs: four never died, two once, one twice, one five times; levels 6-9 reached against 9 before).
  Weak runs are the ones whose shopping skipped attack spells.
- Secrets, the wisp and the graves have not been played by hand on Windows yet.
- `decay_tex` already draws cracks on ordinary walls at depth; keep secret cracks distinguishable if
  textures change (see the plan).
- CI warns that `actions/download-artifact@v6` targets Node 20; bump the action when a newer major is out.
- Boss levels (5, 10, 15, 20) are ordinary levels for now, under their boss's name; there are no
  loop moods yet past level 20, only the numeral.
- The Deep (levels 16-20) is deadly for the autopilot even with every spell at tier 2: eight
  30-minute runs from level 16 died 22 times (25 before the Deep's newcomers came). The Hollows at
  tier 1 died 18 times in eight 50-minute runs from level 11, against 9 before theirs. Tune by hand.
