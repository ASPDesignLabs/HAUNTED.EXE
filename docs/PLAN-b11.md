## What the owner asked for (30 October 2026)

**Part one: adjustments.**

1. **The map moves with you.** The crypt doesn't wait while it is open; you can walk with it out. It shows where you
   have walked apart from the walls you have only seen, and what you can see ahead of you fills in as you look at it.
   It shows the level's own numbers: creatures left, an estimate of the gold still lying, secrets found and
   remaining, and the keys.
2. **Glyphs, not words**, for what a creature takes more or less from, with + and - beside them, so the screen is
   less cluttered; and under its name a glyph for the harm it does, once learnt. What it is weak to, what it shrugs
   off and what it does to you are each learnt apart, per creature and per type.
3. **A sign of the runes**, as the boss abilities' sockets give: on firing, its mark and cooldown for three seconds;
   two seconds before it is ready again, back and flashing; once ready, solid for a second, then gone.

**Part two: how the caster grows** (items, abilities and damage; more ways to make a build of one's own).

4. **Elements**, each carrying its own numbers for the caster (with an innate Efficiency for a class).
5. **An Alignment** in place of spells as wares: a tree for each element where its spell is bought and raised. Fire
   open from the start; Alignment Points one a level; opening a line, two augments of two points bought a point at a
   time at half their bonus a point, an ability, then two paths (one locking out the other), then an Ultimate that
   changes the spell radically. Each point ranks the spell up, a little at first and more later. **Insight**, a new
   trait, shortens casting time.
6. **The owner's two lines**, Electric and Flame (section 9); the other four for us to draft (section 10). Poison is a
   new spell, an arcing projectile whose upgrades leave residue on the floor, then a wider, longer pool for a charged
   cast. Physical is limited by ammunition, may scale harder later, adds nothing to the other elements, and may turn
   armour into a bonus.
7. **Classes**, shaping what spells cost and how well they work.
8. **The soul economy**, which never runs short today: something to manage, even to go without.

## What it touches in the game as it is

**The map** (1.0.0b9; `UI_MAP`, `update_mapmenu`, `render_map`, `map_note`):

- It pauses the crypt, as every menu does (`G.ui`). While it is open W/A/S/D and the left stick pan it, the mouse
  drags it, Space recentres, M, Esc or Tab close it, and the status bar is hidden.
- It fills by distance only: every cell within 3.2 cells (`MAP_REACH`), through walls, marks `G.mapped` (bit 1 the
  shape known, bit 2 been near). Nothing records where you have walked or what you have looked at. The Cartographer
  sets bit 1 everywhere.
- The parchment (`g_scroll`, made once a level by `map_scroll_make` with torn edges, burns and stains) covers the
  middle 240 pixels (`SCROLL_X0`-`SCROLL_X1`), top to bottom.
- The level's numbers exist already, for the tally and the autopilot: `G.kills`, `G.items_got`/`G.items_total`,
  `G.value_total`, `G.links_found`/`G.nlinks`, `G.has_key[]`, and the keys still lying as items.

**The target's readout** (`render_target`, `weak_word`): words either side of its bar, learnt per kind and **spell**
at three kills (`Record.spell_kills[kind][NATK]`, kept through death). What creatures do to the caster is one of
`HURT_BLOW`, `HURT_MAGIC`, `HURT_POISON`, `HURT_STONE` and `HURT_VOID`: how it is resisted, not an element.

**Runes** fire by themselves (`update_defense`, `def_trigger`, `cast_defense`); the only sign is the hand's glow
(`G.rune_glow`, `G.rune_lift`) and `SFX_RUNE`. Cooldowns 40, 27, 20 and 20 s by quality, 16 s Exquisite. The boss
abilities' sockets (`render_sockets`): a 17-pixel bevel, an 11x11 mark, a line filling as it comes round, lower left
over the status bar, a person only. In the dream the experience lines are at the lower right and the face and the
readout at the lower left.

**Spells** (`crawl_gear.c`): nine, bought by tier (about the level sold on), damage a base of 4 to 18 times 1 + 0.25
a tier, a cycle of 0.7 to 3 s, 7% quicker a tier, less Spell Haste. No casting time apart from the hand's rise
(0.22-0.5 s, the spell leaving a third of the way through). Tiers come from the merchant's first pedestal, the
Compendium's spells page, and go at death (all but the fireball), to her claim (a tier), into graves (`Stone.atk`)
and the save. The autopilot buys three attack spells first and picks by weakness and cost (`choose_spell`). Gear
adds Fire, Frost and Lightning (`ST_FIRE`, `ST_FROST`, `ST_SHOCK`, `hit_mult`); the resists are to poison, burning
and slowing. Only Hellfire's floor hurts a creature over time (`Burn`, 2.5 s, eight at once); Frost Shard slows,
Frost Nova freezes; nothing poisons a creature. Holding the button repeats the cast; nothing charges. Chain
Lightning already leaps two to four times, a quarter weaker each.

**Spirit**: the souls, one to six a kill by rank. A cast above tier 1 costs its damage over 150 (0.1 to 1), less
Spirit Cost; tier 1 and below are free. Nothing caps what is carried; at 1.0.0b4 the autopilot netted 14 to 71 a
level on levels 1-8, and 51 of about 10,700 casts went out weak.

**Spectres** come only on levels 1 to 10 of the first descent (`random_type`: `G.level <= 10`), are uncounted and
can't be harmed; in a pocket they come back each time (no slain bit).

**Bats** fly at the face; one touching you for three seconds bites (`contact`), and goes on.

**Points**: ten a Level of Atonement for twelve lines, lost at a death that costs; a cornerstone at each tenth.

**Her hall** (`gen_shop`): her room x 10-20, y 12-17, the wares along its north wall under the sign at (15, 11), the
way on east from x 21; the back room west (x 4-8, the Totem, the Compendium, the Repository); the grave plot south (x
11-21, y 20-26, the stones and the Portal). North of her room is solid rock.

**Saves**: the record is packed by `NATK` and the gear carries spell tiers, so this is save version 7. The caster's
new things go in ledger sections, as `LG_CASTER` did; `LG_CASTER` grows a line (Resist Spirit) and the Codex's stat
bits (`cx_stat`, a u32) outgrow 32 with the new stats.

# Part one

## 1. The map at a walk

M opens the scroll as now, but the crypt goes on: creatures move, missiles fly, the level's clock runs. You walk,
turn, open doors and cast as in play; the map stays centred on you, sliding after you, north up. Other screens (Tab,
B, L, Esc) close it as they open. The dream never opens it.

**Panning** (M1). The arrow keys pan it, a cell a tap and smoothly while held; three seconds after the last pan it
drifts back to you, or at once on Space. On the pad the D-pad pans it, all four ways, a cell a press, and it comes
back to you the same way; the sticks walk and turn. W/A/S/D and the mouse play as without the map.

**Closing it**: M, Esc or Tab on the keyboard; **B** on the pad (M7). While the map is up, B closes it instead of
dodging (the keyboard's double tap still dodges); D-pad up pans north rather than closing it.

**The parchment** (M2, the owner's). The scroll stays solid, but torn through and burnt through in places, a hole or
two near its middle and tears from its edges, about a quarter of it in all, made once a level from its seed as the
stains are (*default*; approved on the sheet). Through a hole the crypt shows, running. Ink that crosses a hole turns
bright and eerie: a pale ghost-light (`R_GHOST` at its top, with a faint violet edge), drifting a pixel now and then
as the overlevels' numbers do, so it seems to float above the paper; on the paper it is the brown ink of now. Marks
and the level's numbers crossing a hole do the same. The status bar comes up over the scroll's foot when you are hurt
or a spell is chosen, as it does without the map.

**Seen and walked.** Two new marks for each cell in `G.mapped`:

- **Walked** (bit 8): every cell the caster's body passes through.
- **Seen** (bit 4): the cells the view's rays cross before they meet a wall, out to where the fog closes, and the
  faces they meet: the raycaster walks those cells for every column already, so it costs a store a cell. A shut door
  stops sight; an open one doesn't; a shut secret still looks like stone (`map_hidden`).

What fills in (M3): what you see, and a ring of 1.5 cells round you through walls; the 3.2-cell reach through walls
goes. Walls are inked wherever a known cell meets stone. A walked cell's floor is washed a shade darker, with a faint
hatch (M4). With the Cartographer the whole level is drawn on arrival, as now, and the wash shows what is left.

**The level's numbers**, a band of ink across the scroll's foot, above the hint:

```
   CREATURES 14     GOLD ABOUT 1,400     SECRETS 2 OF 5     KEYS [gold] [silver] [crimson]
```

- **Creatures**: those the tally counts, still alive, the buried risers and the gargoyles on their plinths among them;
  never the spectres, the Wraith, egg sacs, a boss or its adds. Exact.
- **Gold** (M5): the level's treasure not yet taken, rounded to the nearest hundred (fifty under a thousand), your
  Treasure bonus in it, *ABOUT 1,400*. What creatures drop isn't in it, as it isn't in the tally.
- **Secrets**: links found of all of them, as the tally counts.
- **Keys**: a mark for each colour the level has: solid if held, an outline if still lying.
- In a pocket: its own creatures and gold, and the chain's links found.
- Everyone with the map sees all of it (M6).

## 2. Glyphs on the target

```
                       COLD BAT
                   [bone] [wisp]                 its harm, once learnt (up to two)
   [flake]++ [bolt]+  [##########......]  [drop]-
                   (34/60 in the bar, as now)
```

- **Six glyphs**, 7 by 7 pixels, each in its element's colour (section 4), drawn as the cornerstones' and the boss
  abilities' marks are: a flame, a six-armed flake, a forked bolt, a drop with a bubble in it, a wisp's teardrop flame,
  a bone.
- **Left of the bar**, what it takes more from, the most nearest the bar: ++ at 1.5 times or more, + above plain.
  **Right**, what it takes less from: - below plain, -- at half or less, the glyph struck through where it does
  nothing at all (T1). Up to four a side, the most any creature has.
- A dim ? at the outer end of a side while any element is still to be learnt on this kind. When every element is
  learnt and none differs, that side is empty.
- **Under its name**, the glyphs of the harm it does, up to two (E3), each once learnt; a dim ? before; nothing for a
  creature with no harm of its own (the deacon calls others to fight).
- **Learning** (T2), per kind and element, kept in the record through death: what an element does to a kind, after
  five of that kind are slain by it or twelve of its hits land on that kind, whichever is first; each harm a kind
  does, after it has hurt you with it three times. An old record's kills by spell carry into their elements (Fire
  Bolt's and Hellfire's into Fire, and so on). `CRAWL_LEARNT=1` learns all of it.
- Spirits and bosses as now. The bestiary's pages say the same in glyphs, and the Codex gains a page on the elements.

## 3. Runes in sight

- When a rune fires, its socket comes up: a 17-pixel bevel like the boss abilities', the rune's 11x11 mark in the
  rune's colour, the bevel's edge in its quality's colour, a line along its foot filling as the cooldown comes round.
  No name (R2).
- It stays up three seconds, or while the rune's work lasts if that is longer, the mark glowing until it ends (R4:
  the Bone Ward's bones until broken or gone, the Mirror Veil, Mend, Purify's protection, Time Slip, Soul Reaping,
  Blink's double); then it slides away.
- Two seconds before the rune is ready it comes back, flashing four times a second; the moment it is ready it holds
  solid, its line bright, for a second; then it goes.
- Several stand in a row, the newest outermost, each keeping its place while it shows. In the void a socket shows
  struck through and no rune fires.
- **Where** (R1): the lower right, over the status bar, or in the corner when the bar is away, mirroring the boss
  sockets at the lower left. **In the dream too** (R3): the lower right, the experience lines rising above them.
- Nine marks: Bone Ward (crossed bones), Blink (an eye and a step), Mirror Veil (a hand mirror), Mend (a stitched
  heart), Purify (a drop in a ring), Frost Nova (a burst), Dread (a skull), Time Slip (an hourglass), Soul Reaping (a
  scythe).

| Frost | `R_GLOW` | a flake | Frost Shard | slows | spirit | the Wraith's touch |

**The caster's numbers for each element**: **Rank** (its spell's level, a rank a point spent in its line); **Power**
(more of its damage: Masteries, the class, gear); **Efficiency** (less spirit for its spell; for Physical, a chance a
spear spends no bone: E4); **Insight** (a shorter wind-up for its spell); **Addition** (a share of it added to every
other spell: Electric and the other Masteries, and the gear's Fire, Frost and Lightning additions as now); **Innate**
(what the class gives). Shown on the Alignment screen and as rows on Tab's totals page.

**How they meet** (*default*): they multiply. A cast's spirit is its cost times (1 - Spirit Cost) times (1 -
Efficiency), never under 0.1. Its wind-up is its own times (1 - Insight). Spell Haste shortens the wait between
casts. Damage (worn and bought) and Power add to each other.

**The caster's resists** (E5): as they are (Armour for Physical, Resist Burn for Fire, Resist Poison, Resist Slow for
any slowing), and a new one, **Resist Spirit**: it takes its share off Soul Sap's drain and slowing, and off the
shade's touch, souls and health alike. It comes from amulets and robes (section 5), from the points' *RESIST ALL*
(now +5 to each of four), and from a line of its own beside the other three (+15 for 3 points) (E7). Lightning has
none while nothing deals it. The void still can't be resisted.

## 6. Creatures' new ways (the owner will name this section)

A list of what creatures do with the elements, begun by the owner with Soul Sap. Others may use Soul Sap in time, at
ranks of their own.

**Soul Sap: bats, from rank II** (decided).

- A bat of rank II or higher that stays on your face long enough latches on instead of biting: 3 s at rank II, 2.4 at
  III, 1.8 at IV, 1.2 at V (*defaults*; today any bat bites after 3 s). Rank I bats bite as now.

## 7. Spells as lines: strength, rank, wind-up, charging

**Strength** (A1). A spell's strength follows the Level of Atonement, as a tier followed the crypt's level, and its
rank adds to that: +4% a rank at first, rising to +8% a rank from rank 12. A line opened late still hurts, and a line
fed is clearly stronger. Tuned so that a caster with one line full and a second half full keeps pace with 1.0.0b10's
dream on the same seeds. Physical's ranks climb a little steeper past rank 10.

**Rank** (A2). A line just opened is rank 1; every point spent in it after the opening is a rank more (a line taken
all the way is rank 17 to 20, by its path).

**The wind-up** (K1). The hand gathers a spell before it goes, and you can walk while it does:

- 0.3 s for a light spell at rank 1 (the fireball, the bolt), rising with rank to 0.6 s at the highest;
- up to 0.8 s for the most powerful: the ultimates' casts and the BFS9000; the boss abilities (Swift Dodge, Tangled
  Web, Grave Call, Wraithwalk) stay instant (K4);
- Insight shortens it (each line's Insight, the class's, the bracers'), multiplied;
- Spell Haste still shortens the wait between casts.

The autopilot waits the wind-up too.

**A blow breaks it** (K3, the owner's). A blow that lands while the hand is gathering a spell (or charging one) may
break it: the spell is lost, no spirit or bone is spent, the hand drops, and you may begin again at once. The
caster's stats make it less likely, in the owner's order of weight: Dodge, then Armour, then the resistance to the
blow's element, then Luck. *Defaults*, for the runs to tune:

- The blow's chance: 25%, and twice the share of your health it took (a tenth: 45%; a fifth: 65%), at most 90%.
- Kept back by: Dodge in full, three fifths of the share of the blow your Armour took off, two fifths of your
  resistance to its element (Resist Burn for Fire, Resist Poison, Resist Spirit, Resist Slow for Frost; Physical has
  only Armour), and a quarter of Luck. All of that together keeps back at most nine tenths of the chance.
- A dodged blow touches nothing, as now. What seeps (poison, Soul Sap's drain, the void) and burning underfoot
  never break it; a rock, a claw, a thrown bone or a fireball can.
- A broken charge loses all it had gathered. The autopilot's casts break the same way. Debug: `EVENT windup_broken`.

**Charging** (K2). Once a line has a charging ultimate, holding its spell's button charges it over a second; letting go
releases it; a tap (let go within a quarter of a second) casts the ordinary spell. Repeating by holding is lost for
that spell only. The pad's RT the same. The charge takes the place of the wind-up.

**The charging effect** (the owner's): below the hand, offset so the player can see it, a gathering in the spell's
element: embers swirling inward for Fire, flakes drawn together for Frost, crackling threads for Lightning, green
droplets and bubbles for Poison, violet wisps for Spirit, splinters of bone for Physical. It turns slowly at first and
quickens as the charge fills; full, it pulses steadily. It lights the crypt round you in its colour (a dynamic light
growing with the charge, held while it waits), so shades show in it.

**Physical** costs bones only, never spirit; nothing in its line adds to other elements; armour turns into its damage
(Ossified).

## 9. The owner's lines: Electric and Flame

**Electric** (open 3) > Efficiency (+5% a point, 2) > Insight (-15% a point, 2) > **Ionization** (3) > one path:

- **The spell** (EL2): a bolt from the hand that strikes the creature aimed at at once (today's Chain Lightning
  without its leaps).
- **Ionization**: Electric attacks leave a trail from the hand to what they strike; it hangs for half a second and
  hurts anything that crosses it for half the element's damage, once a creature a trail. Amperage's orb leaves it
  along its flight (EL4).
- Path A: Efficiency II (+10% a point, 2) > **Electric Mastery** (+30% Lightning; 20% Lightning added to every other
  attack and spell; 5) > **Arc** (5): the bolt leaps on to the creature nearest the one it struck, up to four leaps,

- Path B: Insight II (-20% a point, 2) > **Electric Mastery** (+20%; 10% added; 5) > **Amperage** (5): hold to
  charge an orb, slow and large, that flies straight from the hand through what it meets until a wall. Each creature

  full charge, *default*), and from each an arc leaps to exactly one more creature just out of the orb's reach, one
  not yet struck (EL1: nothing changes that count, not Arc, not the Masteries, not gear). Whatever it or its arcs hurt
  can't strike or throw for 1.5 s.

**Flame** (open from the start) > Efficiency (+5% a point, 2) > Insight (-15% a point, 2) > **Firestarter** (3) > one
path:

- **Firestarter**: the fireball sets the floor alight along its flight, the flames lasting 1.5 s; a creature that
  walks through them catches fire.
- **Burning** (FL4): a creature alight burns for two seconds, a quarter of the fireball's damage a second; Burning
  Intensity makes that three and four seconds, Spell Effect longer still. Walking through flames again starts it
  over; it doesn't stack. Firewall's burn and Firestarter's can't both run on one creature: the stronger does.
- Path A: Efficiency II (+10% a point, 2) > **Burning Intensity** (+50% burning time a point, 2) > **Flamewalker**

## 12. Souls

The aim: a caster who casts its best spell at every chance runs dry in a long fight and falls back on weak casts, the
free fireball or the spear; one who spends with thought seldom does; a full vessel is a choice, between power now,
experience, and the bell. Before setting numbers, measure 1.0.0b10: souls gained, spent and carried at each level's
end, and casts gone out weak, over eight seeds from levels 1, 11, 21 and 31.

**The vessel** (S1). Spirit has a ceiling: 20 at first, two more a Level of Atonement (120 at 50), more from the
Sin-Eater, the Vessel stat on gear (section 5) and the Spirit line (*default*: Communion and Spirit Mastery +10 each).
Souls past it slip away: a pale wisp rises from the hand, and once a level *THE VESSEL IS FULL*.

**What a cast costs** (S2). By rank: about 0.2 at rank 1, 1 at rank 10, 2.5 at rank 20, less Efficiency and Spirit
Cost; a full charge 2.5 times a cast; Soul Tether by the second. The fireball (Flame at rank 1) is free. Any spell cast
with too little goes out at rank 1, free; with strength following the Level of Atonement that is weak, not useless.

**What comes in** (S3, both answers). Fewer a kill: about half of today's (half a soul at rank I, one at II, one and a
half at III, two at IV, three at V and gilded; *defaults*, tuned by runs), more with Harvest, three times as many
under Soul Reaping. And they are gathered: a kill leaves its soul as a mote where it fell, hanging for six seconds and
fading in its last two, taken by walking within a cell and a fifth of it. The autopilot takes motes within three steps
of its lane when nothing is on it.

**Sinks.**

  - more creatures (*default* +40%) and more gold (*default* +40% on its treasure);

  - on a boss's level, its waves a third larger and one scrip more when it falls (B2);

    EXPERIENCE*;

- Her *OFFER SOULS* stays beside the font (F2): one turns souls into experience at once, the other makes the next

- Wisps, the altars and the BFS9000, as now.

**Replaying a level at the Accursed Portal** (B1, the owner's). The Portal (`UI_PORTAL`) lists the runs opened and
