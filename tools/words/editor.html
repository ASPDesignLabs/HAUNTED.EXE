What is here

Every string CATACOMB shows a player, read from src/scene_crawl.c and src/crawl_gear.c: captions, menus, hints, cards, the Codex, puzzles and their clues, names and descriptions. One card per distinct string; a string used in several places has its places listed under it. Debug output, the console, the settings window and HAUNT.EXE are not here.

Writing

  • Type in the box under my wording. CATACOMB writes in capitals, so lower case becomes capitals as you type. Curly quotes and long dashes become plain ones: the font has no others.
  • Keep mine marks a string you are happy with, so it counts as done without a rewrite. Flag marks one to come back to. A note is for anything you want me to know.
  • Clearing your box puts my wording back.

Blanks

{1}, {2} and so on are filled in by the game: a number, a name, a word. Under each card it says what fills each one. Use each once, anywhere in the sentence. Some blanks are only a plural ending (an S or nothing); for this pass, leave them where the ending goes, and after it we'll see how the code should handle grammar.

^A is the A button on a controller, drawn in its colour; ^B, ^X, ^Y likewise. In hints, key names (E, TAB, ENTER, SPACE, A/D…) are swapped for buttons when a pad is in use, so keep them as whole words.

Widths

The preview draws your words in the game's font at the size it uses. The orange line is where the space ends; letters past it would be cut off, and show red. Blanks are drawn at a guessed width. Where the code doesn't say how wide the space is, the line is at the screen's width, forty letters, and the real space may be narrower. Eight of my own captions are already too wide and are cut off at both edges.

My habits

You asked for the places where the writing leans on my habits to be marked. These are pattern matches, not judgements, and your own text is checked against them too:

    Profiles

    For each creature, boss, class, spell line, spell, rune and gift: what the game does now, from its tables and code, and fields for what you want it to be: the damage it deals, what it is weak to, how it attacks, what it throws, how it comes at you. Where your choice of element differs from the game's, the line under it turns amber. These fields change nothing in the game by themselves; they are the brief for the mechanics work that follows.

    Saving, and what happens next

    Everything is saved to this page's own database as you type, and I can read it from a Claude session. Only you, and anyone you make an editor, can change it. When you have done a pass, tell me: I'll read your words and traits, move the game's strings into a catalogue that the build reads, put yours in, and check that every string you kept still draws exactly as before.
